1. What this is

Nationwide Media Connect is a peer-to-peer marketplace: we provide the platform where buyers and sellers find each other, chat, and agree deals. We are a venue, not a party to your transactions. Any deal you make is strictly between you and the other user.

2. Eligibility and your account

You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter contracts in Nigeria. You must provide true information: your real name, a working email address you verify with our code, and a real Nigerian phone number. One person may not operate multiple accounts to evade a ban. You are responsible for keeping your password secret and for everything done under your account.

3. Listing rules

List only items you own or are authorised to sell. Describe them honestly — accurate title, price, photos, location, and condition. The following are never allowed: stolen goods, weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods, adult services, and anything illegal under Nigerian law. We may remove any listing that breaks these rules without notice.

4. Trading safely

Meet in a public place. Inspect the item before you pay. Never pay in advance for an item you have not seen. We are not responsible for the quality, safety, legality, or delivery of items, or for payments between users. If a deal goes wrong, that dispute is between you and the other party — though you can report them to us and we will review their account.

If your account is banned for fraud, spam, fake listings, or ban evasion, we may also ban your device fingerprints and your phone number, which blocks new accounts from the same device or number. If you believe a ban was a mistake, message us on WhatsApp at 09150951870 to appeal. Appeals are reviewed manually.
