1. Who we are

Nationwide Media Connect ("we", "us") is a peer-to-peer marketplace operated by Skepter Forge in Lagos, Nigeria. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. It is written to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

2. Data we collect

Account data. When you sign up we collect your full name, email address, phone number, and a password (stored only as a secure hash — we never see your actual password). Your email is verified with a one-time code before your account is activated.

Device fingerprints. To enforce bans against scammers who create new accounts, we compute and store three anonymous hashes of your device: a full browser fingerprint, a hardware fingerprint including your graphics chip, and a hardware fingerprint excluding it. These hashes cannot be reversed into personal information. If a device is banned, any account signing in from it is blocked automatically.

Listings. Anything you publish — title, description, price, category, photos, location, and condition — is visible to every user of the app and to anyone you share the listing link with.

Chats. Messages you send to buyers or sellers (text and photos) are stored so both sides can read the conversation. Photos you send in chat are stored in our image storage.

Profile. Your display name, avatar photo, verification tier, and ratings given by other users are visible to other users.

Reports. If you report a user or listing, we store your report and the reason.

Technical data. Like most apps, we keep basic logs needed to run the service (for example, when your account was created and when listings were posted).

3. Why we collect it

We use your data only to run the marketplace: create and secure your account, verify you are a real person, display your listings and profile to other users, deliver chat messages, enforce our ban system against fraud, and respond to reports and support requests. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who can see your data

Your listings, profile name, avatar, and tier are public inside the app. Your chat messages are visible only to you and the other person in the conversation. Your email address, password hash, and device fingerprints are private and are never shown to other users. We disclose data to law enforcement only when required by Nigerian law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, listings, and chats within 30 days. Device ban records linked to banned accounts may be kept longer where needed to prevent banned users from returning, as permitted by law.

6. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, message us on WhatsApp at 09150951870 or DM @adanskepter on Instagram. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is stored with Supabase using encrypted connections, and sensitive admin actions require a secret key. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children

Nationwide Media Connect is not intended for children under 18. If you are under 18, please do not create an account.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app grows. The version number and date at the top will change, and continued use of the app after an update means you accept the new version.
