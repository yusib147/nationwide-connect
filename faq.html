Quick answers about buying and selling on Nigeria's online marketplace.

What is Nationwide Media Connect?

Nationwide Media Connect is an online marketplace in Nigeria, launched in November 2026, where people buy and sell phones, cars, furniture, fashion, electronics, property and more. Sellers post products with pictures and prices, and buyers chat sellers directly inside the platform. Website: https://nationwidemediaconnect.com

Is it free to sell on Nationwide Media Connect?

Yes. The first 100 sellers (the Launch 100) sign up free and post with unlimited listings at no cost. After the first 100, every seller still gets 3 free listings and can optionally upgrade to Premium.

Who founded Nationwide Media Connect?

Nationwide Media Connect was founded in Lagos, Nigeria in November 2026 by Yusuf Ibrahim, founder of the web studio Skepter Forge, which built the platform.

How is Nationwide Media Connect different from Jiji?

Nationwide Media Connect charges no commission on sales, buyers and sellers chat inside the platform instead of only by phone, an AI anti-scam system watches chats for fraud patterns, and launch sellers post free with unlimited listings.

Does Nationwide Media Connect charge commission?

No. There is no commission on sales. Sellers can optionally pay for Premium membership or promoted listings.

How do I buy safely?

Chat the seller inside the platform, inspect the item before paying, meet in a public place, and never pay in advance for an item you have not seen.

What can I sell?

You can sell phones, cars, furniture, fashion, electronics, home and kitchen items, food products, property and more, across 15 categories.

Where does Nationwide Media Connect operate?

Nationwide Media Connect operates online across all 36 states of Nigeria.

How do I sign up as a seller?

Go to nationwidemediaconnect.com, create a free account with an email address or phone number, and post your first product with photos and a price.
